About

I'm Joanna, a videographer and visual artist obsessed with atmosphere and emotion. My work exists somewhere between documentary and dream — grounded in real people and real moments, with a touch of magic layered on top.

I've shot and edited projects for various clients, from intimate portraits to live events and music videos. I care deeply about honesty, texture, and the tiny details that make a frame feel alive.

What I Love Working On

  • Music videos & performance films
  • Documentary & short-form storytelling
  • Experimental, artsy visual pieces
  • Live sessions & event coverage
  • Creative collaborations
