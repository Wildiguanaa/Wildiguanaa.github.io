About

I'm a filmmaker and emerging-tech creative working with cinema, interactive media, and socially grounded storytelling. I graduated Magna Cum Laude from Binghamton University with a BA in Cinema and minors in Digital & Data Studies and Genocide & Mass Atrocity Prevention.

My experience spans 3D printing, lidar scanning, VR/AR, animation, editing, broadcast, and development for documentary and scripted media. I've worked at Binghamton's Emerging Technology Studio, contributed to the TLC docuseries Jay and Pamela, collaborated internationally as a Global Intern for IFIT in Barcelona, and created interactive projection art for LUMA Festival.

I love building work that blends the technical and the poetic. Outside the studio, I enjoy biking, camping, making homemade ice cream, and hanging out with my golden-doodle, Brooklynn.
