About

I’m a filmmaker and emerging-tech creative working with cinema, interactive media, and socially grounded storytelling. I graduated Magna Cum Laude from Binghamton University with a BA in Cinema and minors in Digital & Data Studies and Genocide & Mass Atrocity Prevention.

My experience spans 3D printing, lidar scanning, VR/AR, animation, editing, broadcast, and development work for both documentary and scripted media. I’ve worked as a technician at Binghamton’s Emerging Technology Studio, contributed to the TLC docuseries Jay and Pamela, collaborated internationally as a Global Intern for the Institute For Integrated Transitions in Barcelona, and created interactive projection art for the LUMA Festival. I also helped lead the Student Experimental Film Festival, securing funding and designing custom 3D-printed awards.

I love building work that blends the technical and the poetic; projects that live between reality and reverie. Outside the studio, I enjoy art, biking, camping, making homemade ice cream, and hanging out with my mischievous golden-doodle, Brooklynn.
